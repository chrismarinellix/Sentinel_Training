🎓 Vysus University

Career Progression Framework

Formal criteria for advancing through engineering levels

About the University Program

The Vysus University program formalizes the career progression pathway for Grid Connection Engineers. Advancement through each level requires demonstrated competency in both technical skills and professional capabilities. Promotion assessments are conducted annually.

Annual Assessment Cycle

Promotion assessments are conducted once per year. Engineers who meet the criteria for the next level will be invited to complete the formal assessment process, including a technical test and panel review.

Entry → Senior

Technical Requirements

  • Completed all Entry Level training modules (minimum 80% average score)
  • Demonstrated understanding of NER Chapter 5 connection process
  • Contributed to at least 3 GPS submissions under supervision
  • Basic proficiency in PSS/E or PSCAD simulation software
  • Completed at least 2 power system studies (load flow, fault analysis)

Professional Requirements

  • Minimum 18 months experience at Entry Level
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently on defined tasks
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Positive feedback from project leads and clients
  • Active participation in team knowledge sharing

Assessment Components

  • Technical Test: Senior Engineer Technical Assessment (see Tests section)
  • Portfolio Review: Documentation of project contributions
  • Panel Interview: 45-minute review with Lead/Principal engineers

Senior → Lead

Technical Requirements

  • Completed all Senior Level training modules (minimum 85% average score)
  • Led at least 5 GPS submissions as primary engineer
  • Advanced proficiency in PSS/E and PSCAD simulation software
  • Demonstrated expertise in at least 2 specialist areas (harmonics, protection, system strength, etc.)
  • Successfully managed AEMO RFI processes independently
  • Contributed to development of technical procedures or guidelines

Professional Requirements

  • Minimum 3 years experience at Senior Level
  • Demonstrated mentoring of junior engineers
  • Led client meetings and technical presentations
  • Managed project budgets and schedules independently
  • Strong stakeholder management skills (AEMO, NSPs, clients)

Assessment Components

  • Technical Test: Lead Engineer Technical Assessment (see Tests section)
  • Case Study Presentation: 30-minute presentation on complex project
  • Leadership Assessment: 360-degree feedback from team members
  • Panel Interview: 60-minute review with Principal engineers

Lead → Principal

Technical Requirements

  • Completed all Lead Level training modules (minimum 90% average score)
  • Recognised as technical authority in at least one major discipline
  • Contributed to industry standards development or regulatory submissions
  • Published technical papers or presented at industry conferences
  • Designed innovative solutions to complex technical challenges
  • Established as trusted advisor for major client accounts

Professional Requirements

  • Minimum 4 years experience at Lead Level
  • Successfully developed team members to Senior/Lead levels
  • Strategic contribution to business development
  • P&L responsibility or significant commercial oversight
  • Industry recognition and external network

Assessment Components

  • Technical Test: Principal Engineer Technical Assessment (see Tests section)
  • Strategic Presentation: Vision for technical discipline or market area
  • Business Case: Demonstrated commercial impact and client development
  • Executive Interview: 90-minute review with Director and Principal panel

📋 Promotion Tests

Formal Assessment for Level Progression

Comprehensive evaluations developed by Principal Engineers

About Promotion Tests

These formal assessments are designed to evaluate an engineer's readiness for promotion to the next level. Each test has been developed by the Principal Engineers in the Grid Connection Engineering group to ensure comprehensive coverage of the technical and professional competencies required at each level.

Prerequisites

Before taking a promotion test, engineers must have:

  • Completed the required training modules for their current level
  • Met the minimum experience requirements outlined in the University section
  • Received approval from their Line Manager to proceed with assessment
  • Spent adequate time on projects developing practical skills

How Tests Are Administered

Promotion tests are conducted under supervised conditions. The test is sent to the candidate by their Line Manager, and must be completed within the allocated time. Results are reviewed by the assessment panel before the panel interview.
